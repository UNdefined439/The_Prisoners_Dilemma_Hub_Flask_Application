Play

Each game runs for ten rounds against a single opponent. Sometimes you'll be told exactly who you're up against, like the Tit for Tat strategy in the Basics game below — other times they'll only be listed as Mystery, and it's on you to work out their pattern from how they respond to you. Either way, the goal is the same: identify the strategy and exploit it before the ten rounds run out. Win or lose, the opponent's identity and the logic behind their strategy get revealed once the game ends. One thing worth sitting with as you play, though — beating your opponent and winning the game aren't necessarily the same thing.
